# The Unbroken City

A 70-second animated documentary about the siege of Constantinople (717–718).
1080×1920, 30 FPS, H.264 video, stereo AAC, with an original instrumental score.

## Downloads

- [Finished documentary MP4](https://github.com/GeoTouhme/geo/raw/refs/heads/main/downloads/historical_documentary.mp4)
- [Editable project ZIP](https://github.com/GeoTouhme/geo/raw/refs/heads/main/downloads/historical-motion-source.zip)
- [Scene contact sheet](downloads/storyboard-contact-sheet.jpg)

The ZIP includes the Remotion/React/TypeScript source, local assets, original
score, structured historical research, storyboard, measured beat map, rendering
instructions, credits, licenses, and quality-control evidence.

## Validation and research scope

All 2,100 frames decoded successfully. Stereo audio alignment was verified,
and one frame from each of the 18 scenes was extracted from the final MP4 and
visually inspected. No unexpected blank frames were found before the intentional
closing fade.

Historical claims were checked against the Wikipedia article text supplied by
the user. Independent external reference verification was limited by network
access. Disputed troop and casualty figures are omitted. Military diagrams are
schematic; the map uses a modern physical coastline for geographic orientation,
without claiming historical political boundaries.
